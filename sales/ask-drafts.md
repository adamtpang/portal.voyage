## The post (r/digitalnomad, r/expats, Show HN, IndieHackers)

> Every "best cities for digital nomads" list ranks the same 10 cities the same way for
> everyone. Your passport, your career, and your budget change which city actually wins, and
> none of those lists ask.
>
> I built a tool that does. Set your passport, what you do for work, and what you're
> optimizing for, and it ranks 30 cities across three axes: cost of living, career upside, and
> people (how easy it is to find your scene). Move the sliders and the ranking changes live,
> because the honest answer is "it depends what you weight," not a fixed top 10.
>
> Each city also shows a visa-feasibility read for your specific passport, so you're not
> three tabs deep in immigration forums before you find out a city that scores well is actually
> hard to get into.
>
> Free to use at portal.voyage, no signup to see your ranking.
